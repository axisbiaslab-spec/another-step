```en
The greatest battles know no witnesses; they unfold in the silence of the heart. Our duty is simple: to be sincere.

Let us open our hearts; commit the act that Reason demands. The outcome of the deed belongs to Fortune and is not within our power, but the purity of our intent belongs to us eternally.

We write these lines not to return the past, but for the honor of the present.
```
