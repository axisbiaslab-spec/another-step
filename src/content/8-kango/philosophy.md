```en
Do we feel how easy it has become to breathe? The stone of guilt has fallen, for we acted in accordance with Nature.

True joy does not come from without—it is born of a clear conscience. This is the ultimate intoxication with Freedom, when we become masters of ourselves.

We do not mourn what has been; we celebrate life, for the greatness of the spirit renders all hardships insignificant.
```
