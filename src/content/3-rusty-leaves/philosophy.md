Our plans have turned to dust, and we have touched the bottom. Let us thank Fate for this, for only on solid ground can we build something unshakable.

Ruin is a great teacher; it tears the veil of illusions from our eyes. Let us remember the dogma: events in themselves are mute; only our judgment gives them a voice.
